The horseman

Raised on an Oklahoma ranch. Schooled by Europe's finest teamsters.

Shawn Howard grew up ranching in Oklahoma and rodeoed as a young man, riding bulls and roping. Today he is one of the nation's premier stagecoach drivers, a citizen of the Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma, and a working link to the traditions of the American West.

His path to the driver's seat ran through Europe. Recruited as a stuntman for the Disney-hosted Buffalo Bill's Wild West Show, he spent more than twenty years studying under European teamsters and Combined Driving champions between stunt jobs, learning the craft of putting four, six and more horses to work as one.

That apprenticeship turned into a career on both sides of the Atlantic: driving stunts for film and television, arena performances in front of rodeo's biggest crowds, and training driving teams for Combined Driving competitors, movie producers and Wells Fargo.

Driving clinics

Learn to drive from the box seat.

Shawn's two-day driving clinics keep groups small, five participants a day, so every driver gets one-on-one time. Bring your own horse or team, or work with one of the host's.

  • The basics: harnessing, hooking up, troubleshooting and tuning up
  • Starting and training driving horses
  • Harness tack, collars and bits
  • Tips, tricks and open discussion

Founder

Professional Indian Relay League

“Build a professional stage for Indian Relay while keeping Native culture, riders, families, and horses at the center.”

Shawn founded PIRL, a nonprofit league based in Ringling, Oklahoma, to organize and elevate Indian Relay Racing, drawing on a career at the intersection of Indigenous culture, equestrian performance, film, live entertainment and sports storytelling.
